CASE 01 / 04

NeuroSoc

Neuromorphic Security Operations Center

◆ Business problem

SOC analysts triage tens of thousands of alerts daily, and legacy signature engines fire false positives on the majority of them. The cost isn't the tooling — it's the analyst hours burned and the genuine intrusion that slips past a fatigued queue. A single missed breach in the simulated enterprise carried a $2.3M exposure.

◆ Technical approach

Spiking neural networks (snnTorch) encode network telemetry as spike trains so temporal attack signatures are native to the model, not bolted on with sliding windows. Liquid neural nets handle concept drift; XGBoost provides a calibrated second opinion. Federated learning trains across tenants without moving raw logs, and lattice-based crypto keeps the alert bus quantum-resistant. Shipped on Docker + Kubernetes behind FastAPI with a React console.

CASE 02 / 04

NeuroFlux

Unsupervised SNN framework for biomedical signal integrity

◆ Business problem

50/60 Hz powerline interference corrupts ECG, EEG, and PPG traces on low-cost clinical hardware. Conventional notch filters need the interference frequency known in advance and distort the R-peak morphology clinicians diagnose from — forcing repeat acquisitions and eroding trust in edge devices.

◆ Technical approach

Two mechanisms in one network. The passive path — LIF membrane filtering, Poisson rate encoding, population decoding — preserves diagnostic landmarks at full fidelity. Trace-based STDP then adds amplitude-domain SNR recovery in the low-SNR regime (≤ 0 dB). No labels, no reference noise channel, no prior on the interference frequency. Numba-JIT hot loops, MLflow lineage, property-based tests.

CASE 03 / 04

Astraeus

Orbital network digital twin · RL + GNN scheduler

◆ Business problem

Ground-station contact windows are the scarcest resource in a satellite constellation. Greedy and heuristic schedulers leave downlink capacity stranded whenever thousands of assets contend for the same passes — every wasted window is paid-for bandwidth that never sells.

◆ Technical approach

Model the constellation as a dynamic graph and let a GNN encode topology while a PPO agent (Stable-Baselines3) learns the scheduling policy over 100,000 episodes. Skyfield supplies real orbital mechanics for the twin; Flask + Socket.IO streams live telemetry into a CesiumJS 3D globe so operators see the policy act rather than read a log.

CASE 04 / 04

KisaanMitra

Agricultural multi-agent recommendation platform

◆ Business problem

Smallholder farming decisions — crop choice, irrigation timing, input spend, market window — each depend on a different data domain, and a single monolithic model gives advice that is confidently wrong on the constraint that actually binds (water budget, soil pH, or price floor).

◆ Technical approach

Seven specialist agents — soil, weather, crop, irrigation, pest, market, and an orchestrator — communicate over RabbitMQ using the Model Context Protocol, with a constraint-aware optimizer reconciling their proposals before anything reaches the farmer. FastAPI microservices, TimescaleDB for high-frequency sensor ingest, Dockerized across a 9-phase delivery plan.
